Phone Answering Guide

JMM Construction · 347-228-4257

1. How to answer

"Thank you for calling JMM Construction, this is [your name] — how can I help you?"

Smile when you say it — people can hear it. Speak slowly and clearly.

2. Find out what they need

Let them explain, then guide the conversation. You're collecting the info on the lead sheet (page 2). Ask these, in a natural order:

  1. "Can I get your name?"
  2. "What's the best number to reach you back at?" (repeat it back to confirm)
  3. "What kind of work are you looking to have done?" (carpentry, tile, concrete, painting, taping, or a full remodel)
  4. "Is this interior or exterior? Residential or commercial?"
  5. "Where's the job located — which borough / neighborhood?"
  6. "Can you tell me a little about the size or scope? (one room, whole floor, etc.)"
  7. "When are you hoping to get started?"
  8. "How did you hear about us?" (flyer, referral, Instagram)

3. Answers to common questions

"How much will it cost?"
"We give free estimates. The best way is to have someone take a look or go over the details — I'll set that up. Prices are honest and fair."
"Are you licensed & insured?"
"Yes — we're a NYC-licensed contractor and fully insured."
"What areas do you cover?"
"All 5 boroughs and the tri-state area."
"How long have you been doing this?"
"Over 24 years of experience."
"Do you do [specific job]?"
If it's carpentry, tile, concrete, painting, taping, flooring, or remodeling — "Yes, we do." If unsure — "Let me check with the team and call you right back."
"Can you come today / right now?"
"Let me get your info and I'll check the schedule and get right back to you."

4. Close every call the same way

"Great — I've got your info. We'll get back to you [today / within 24 hours] to set up your free estimate. Thanks for calling JMM Construction!"

Tips

  • Always write it down — even if you think you'll remember. Use the lead sheet.
  • Repeat the phone number back to make sure it's right. A wrong number = a lost job.
  • If you can't answer something, never guess — say "I'll find out and call you right back," and do.
  • If you miss a call, call back as soon as you can — fast callbacks win jobs.
  • Be friendly and unhurried. You represent the company.
Keep this by the phone. Questions on a job's details? Check with your dad before quoting anything.
